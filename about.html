About VivaVoltia

VivaVoltia is an independent website that explains residential solar energy for homeowners in the United States. Solar is a big purchase, and sales pitches often make it hard to compare options. We publish plain-English guides on solar panels, inverters, home batteries, costs, and incentives so you can ask better questions before you sign anything.

What We Cover

  • How solar equipment works and how to compare panels, inverters, and batteries.
  • Realistic system costs and payback periods.
  • Federal, state, and utility rules that affect your savings, such as net metering and tax credits.
  • Practical questions like HOA rules, maintenance, roof types, and power outages.

How We Research Our Guides

Our articles are written and reviewed by the VivaVoltia editorial team. We rely on public, verifiable sources: the IRS and U.S. Department of Energy for tax and policy topics, state energy offices and the DSIRE incentives database for local programs, and manufacturer datasheets for equipment specifications. Prices are national averages and ranges, not quotes, and your local price may differ.

Energy policy changes often. When a rule changes, as it did when the 30% federal homeowner tax credit ended on December 31, 2025, we update the affected articles and show the review date at the top of each guide.

Independence and Advertising

VivaVoltia is free to read and is funded by display advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers do not pay for coverage and have no say in what we write. We do not sell solar systems or install them, and we do not currently share your details with installers.

Corrections and Feedback

If you spot an error or an outdated figure, please tell us through our contact page. We review every correction request and update the article when it is warranted.
