How This Calculator Works

First, it converts your monthly bill into yearly electricity use (bill ÷ price per kWh × 12). Then it sizes a system that produces that much energy, based on the average peak sun hours in your region. Cost is system size × installed price per watt, minus any rebates you enter. Savings grow each year with electricity prices, and shrink slightly as panels age.
