If you have a metal roof, you're in luck. Metal is one of the best surfaces for solar panels: it lasts as long as or longer than the panels, it's strong, and on standing seam roofs panels can be attached without drilling a single hole.

Why Metal Roofs and Solar Work Well Together

  • Longevity: metal roofs commonly last 40–70 years, so you're unlikely to need to remove panels for a re-roof during their 25–30 year life. Asphalt shingles often last 20–30 years.
  • Fewer or no penetrations: on standing seam roofs, panels clamp onto the seams.
  • Strength: metal roofs handle the added load well and shed snow easily.
  • Heat reflection: some metal roofs stay cooler than dark shingles, which helps panel efficiency slightly.

Standing seam: the ideal case

Standing seam roofs have raised vertical seams joining the panels. Seam clamps grip those seams with set screws, giving a strong attachment without piercing the roof. That preserves the roof's weather seal and often its warranty. Labor tends to be faster, which can lower installation costs.

Corrugated and exposed-fastener roofs

These roofs require attachments that penetrate the metal into the structure below. Done properly, with mounts designed for the profile and rated sealing systems, they are reliable. Ask what sealing method the installer uses and whether it's covered by their workmanship warranty.

Things to Check Before Installing

  1. Roof warranty: ask the roof manufacturer or installer which attachment methods keep the warranty valid.
  2. Seam profile: clamps must match your specific seam shape; installers can identify it.
  3. Grounding: metal roofs and racking must be properly bonded and grounded per electrical code.
  4. Snow guards: metal roofs shed snow quickly; panels can change how snow slides, so snow retention may be needed over walkways and entries.
  5. Installer experience: choose an installer who has done metal roofs before and can show examples.

Cost

System prices on metal roofs are generally similar to or slightly below those on shingle roofs, especially for standing seam, where installation is faster. Corrugated and stone-coated roofs can cost a bit more because of specialized hardware. Overall system cost follows the same 2026 ranges covered in our cost guide. Frequently Asked Questions

Should I replace my shingle roof with metal before going solar?

If your roof needs replacing within about 10 years, doing it before solar is sensible, and metal is a long-lasting option. It costs more upfront than shingles.

Do panels make metal roofs noisier in rain?

Panels can actually soften rain noise on the covered area, since raindrops hit the glass first.
