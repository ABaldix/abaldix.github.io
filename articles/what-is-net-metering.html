What Is Net Metering? How to Sell Excess Power Back to the Grid

👨‍🔧

By Alex Sterling

Senior Solar Energy Consultant • Fact-checked

During a sunny summer afternoon, your solar panels will likely produce much more electricity than your home is actively using. Without a battery, where does that extra power go? It gets sent back to your local power grid. Net metering is the billing mechanism that pays you for that electricity.

The Mechanics of Net Metering

When your solar panels produce excess energy, your electricity meter physically spins backwards. Your utility company credits your account for the electricity you provided. Then, at night when your panels are dormant, you pull electricity from the grid using those accumulated credits.

At the end of the billing cycle, you are only billed for your "net" energy use (Total Energy Pulled from Grid - Total Energy Sent to Grid).

State Policies Matter: Not all states offer 1-to-1 net metering (where they pay you the same retail rate they charge you). Some states offer lower wholesale rates for your excess power. Check your specific state and utility company policies.
