Can Your HOA Stop You From Installing Solar Panels?

👨‍🔧

By Alex Sterling

Senior Solar Energy Consultant • Fact-checked

Over 74 million Americans live in communities governed by a Homeowner Association (HOA). Historically, HOAs were notorious for blocking solar panel installations due to "aesthetic concerns." But if you want to switch to solar in 2026, the law is likely on your side.

Solar Access Rights Laws

Currently, more than half of all US states have enacted Solar Access Rights laws. States like California, Texas, Florida, and Colorado have strict legislation that explicitly prevents HOAs from outright banning homeowners from installing solar panels.

What Can the HOA Actually Control?

Even with Solar Access laws, your HOA still has some power. They can place "reasonable restrictions" on your installation. For example:

  • They can require you to install the panels on the back of your roof (hidden from the street), but only if it does not significantly decrease the system's efficiency or increase the cost.
  • They can mandate that wiring and conduits be painted to match the color of your home.
  • They can require you to submit an architectural review form before installation begins.
How to Win: Have your solar installation company handle the HOA. Professional installers deal with HOAs every day. They will provide the engineering plans and legal documentation required to get your system approved without headaches.
