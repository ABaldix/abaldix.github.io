Tesla Powerwall 3 vs FranklinWH aPower 2: Which Whole-Home Battery Wins?

Both batteries can back up a whole home on their own. Tesla integrates the solar inverter; FranklinWH works with almost any solar system and a generator. Here is how they compare.

VivaVoltia Editorial Team Updated October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Key Takeaways

  • The FranklinWH aPower 2 stores more (15 kWh vs. 13.5 kWh); the Powerwall 3 delivers more continuous power (11.5 kW vs. 10 kW).
  • FranklinWH offers a 15-year warranty and native generator integration through its aGate controller.
  • Powerwall 3 includes a solar inverter, so it is usually cheaper for new solar-plus-storage installations.
  • FranklinWH is AC-coupled and inverter-agnostic, which makes it a strong choice for adding storage to existing solar.

If you want one battery that can carry most of a house through an outage, the Powerwall 3 vs FranklinWH aPower 2 is the matchup to look at. Both use LFP cells, both deliver around 10 kW or more of continuous power, and both can start a central air conditioner. The real differences are in how they connect to solar, how they handle generators, how long they are warranted, and what they cost in your situation.

Capacity and Power

The aPower 2 holds about 11% more energy, which means a bit more runtime during an outage. The Powerwall 3 has slightly higher continuous output, which helps when several large loads run at once. In practice, both can usually start a 4- to 5-ton air conditioner, run a well pump, and keep essential circuits on. For long outages or all-electric homes, either system can be expanded. FranklinWH's ceiling is unusually high at 15 batteries per aGate.

Solar Compatibility: The Biggest Difference

Powerwall 3 is a hybrid battery: your panels connect directly to its built-in inverter. In a new installation, that removes the cost of a separate string inverter, which is why it is often the lower-cost option for new solar-plus-storage. For existing solar, it can be added in AC-coupled configurations, but you pay for an inverter you may not need.

FranklinWH aPower 2 is AC-coupled and inverter-agnostic. It works alongside microinverters, string inverters, and most existing systems, which makes it a strong retrofit choice. FranklinWH also sells a hybrid model with direct solar input for new installs. Our hybrid vs. grid-tie inverter guide explains the trade-offs.

Generators and Smart Circuits

FranklinWH's aGate controller is built to integrate a standby or portable generator, so the generator can recharge the battery during a long outage. It also includes smart circuit control to shed loads automatically. That makes FranklinWH attractive in rural areas and places with multi-day storms. Tesla's system focuses on solar and grid charging and does not offer the same native generator integration.

Warranty and Long-Term Value

FranklinWH's 15-year warranty, with a 60 MWh throughput cap, covers five more years than Tesla's 10-year warranty. If you cycle your battery daily under time-of-use rates or net billing, the longer coverage has real value. Both companies have large installer networks in the US, and both batteries use LFP cells known for long cycle life.

Cost: Which Is Cheaper?

For new solar plus one battery, the Powerwall 3 is usually cheaper overall because the inverter is included. For adding storage to existing solar, quotes are often closer, and FranklinWH's extra capacity and warranty can tip the value its way. Always compare total installed prices, including electrical panel work and any required upgrades. See how much a solar battery costs for state incentives that may apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FranklinWH better than Powerwall 3?

Neither is better in every case. FranklinWH has more capacity, a longer warranty, and native generator support. Powerwall 3 has slightly more power and is usually cheaper for new solar installations because its inverter is built in.

Can FranklinWH work with my existing solar panels?

Usually, yes. The aPower 2 is AC-coupled and designed to work with most existing solar inverters, including microinverter systems.

Can a Powerwall 3 be charged by a generator?

Tesla's system is designed around solar and grid charging and does not offer the native generator integration that FranklinWH's aGate provides. Ask your installer about any workaround they support.

How long will one battery run my home?

It depends on your loads. A home averaging 1 kW overnight could run about 13 hours on a Powerwall 3 or 15 hours on an aPower 2. Air conditioning or electric heat shortens that considerably.
