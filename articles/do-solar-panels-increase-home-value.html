Do Solar Panels Increase Home Value in the US?

☀️

By the VivaVoltia Editorial Team

Last reviewed: October 2026

If you are considering moving in the next 5 to 10 years, you might wonder if installing a solar array is a sunk cost. Will buyers pay a premium for a home with solar, or will it make your house harder to sell?

The Zillow Data

Extensive studies by Zillow and the National Bureau of Economic Research have consistently shown that homes with solar panels sell for a premium. On average, a fully owned solar system increases a home's value by 4.1% across the United States. For a $400,000 home, that translates to a $16,400 increase in equity.

The Catch: You Must Own the Panels

This property value boost only applies if you own the system (paid in cash or financed with a loan). If you have a Solar Lease or a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA), the panels do not add equity. In fact, a lease can sometimes deter buyers who do not want to inherit a long-term energy contract.

Property Tax Exemptions: In many states, the added value from a solar installation is exempt from property tax assessments, meaning your home value goes up, but your property taxes do not.
