How Many Solar Panels Do I Need to Power My House?

👨‍🔧

By Alex Sterling

Senior Solar Energy Consultant • Fact-checked

Determining the exact number of solar panels you need comes down to three main variables: your annual energy usage (measured in kWh), the wattage of the panels you choose, and the amount of peak sunlight your zip code receives.

The Simple Calculation Method

The average US home consumes about 10,500 kWh of electricity per year. If you purchase premium 400-watt monocrystalline panels and live in an area with average sunlight, you will generally need between 18 and 22 solar panels to offset 100% of your usage.
