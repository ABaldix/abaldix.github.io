REC vs Qcells Solar Panels (2026): Alpha Pure-RX vs Q.TRON Compared

REC and Qcells make two of the most recommended premium panels in the US. On paper they are close, but they differ in cell technology, heat tolerance, warranty terms, and origin. Here is how to choose.

VivaVoltia Editorial Team Updated October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Key Takeaways

  • The REC Alpha Pure-RX reaches up to 470 W and 22.6% efficiency; the Qcells Q.TRON BLK M-G2+ reaches up to 440 W and 22.5%.
  • REC's heterojunction cells lose less output in heat (−0.24%/°C vs. about −0.30%/°C), which matters on hot roofs.
  • Both carry 25-year product and performance warranties; REC guarantees 92% output at year 25 vs. about 90.6% for Qcells.
  • Qcells assembles Q.TRON panels for the US market in Georgia; REC builds the Alpha Pure-RX in Singapore.

REC vs Qcells is one of the most common decisions homeowners face when comparing premium solar quotes. Both brands are trusted by installers, both offer 25-year warranties, and their headline efficiency numbers are within a few tenths of a percent. The differences that matter show up in hot weather, in long-term output guarantees, in how much fits on a small roof, and in where the panels are made.

Efficiency and Roof Space

Efficiency figures are nearly identical, so for most roofs either panel will deliver similar production per square foot. The REC's higher wattage per panel means you need slightly fewer modules for the same system size: about 17 REC 470 W panels versus 19 Qcells 430 W panels for roughly 8 kW. That can help on small or cut-up roofs with vents, dormers, and fire setbacks. Learn more in our guide to monocrystalline panels.

Heat Performance

All panels lose output as they heat up. A temperature coefficient of −0.24%/°C means the REC loses 0.24% of its power for every degree Celsius above 25°C (77°F); the Qcells loses about 0.30%. On a summer roof where cells reach 65°C, that is roughly a 10% loss for REC versus 12% for Qcells. Across a hot climate like Arizona, Texas, or Southern California, the gap adds up to a percent or two of yearly production. In mild or cold climates, it hardly matters. See why heat matters in Arizona.

Warranties and Degradation

Both brands cover the product and its performance for 25 years. REC guarantees at least 92% of rated output after 25 years, with 0.25% maximum yearly degradation after the first year. Qcells guarantees about 90.6% at year 25. REC also offers its ProTrust warranty, which can include labor coverage when you use a REC Certified installer. Ask each installer exactly which warranty package applies to your system. Our guide on how long solar panels last explains degradation in more detail.

Price and Availability

Installed prices for systems using either brand are usually close. EnergySage's 2026 rankings showed average marketplace prices of about $2.45 per watt for REC Alpha Pure-RX systems and $2.43 per watt for Qcells Q.TRON systems. Wholesale panel prices move with tariffs and supply, so one brand may be cheaper from a given installer. Compare full quotes per watt rather than panel prices alone, using our quote comparison guide.

Made in USA: Does It Matter?

Qcells assembles Q.TRON panels for the US market at its factory in Dalton, Georgia, which appeals to buyers who prefer domestic manufacturing and can matter for some commercial incentives tied to domestic content. For a homeowner buying a system outright in 2026, where the panel is made does not change any federal incentive, because the homeowner credit has ended. It can still matter to leasing companies that claim commercial credits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is more efficient, REC or Qcells?

They are nearly tied. The REC Alpha Pure-RX tops out at 22.6% efficiency, and the Qcells Q.TRON BLK M-G2+ at 22.5%. REC performs better in high heat thanks to its lower temperature coefficient.

Are Qcells panels made in the USA?

Qcells assembles Q.TRON panels for the US market at its factory in Dalton, Georgia. REC manufactures the Alpha Pure-RX in Singapore.

Which has the better warranty?

Both offer 25-year product and performance warranties. REC guarantees a higher output at year 25 (92% vs. about 90.6%) and offers labor coverage through certified installers.

Is REC or Qcells better for hot climates?

REC has the edge in hot climates because its heterojunction cells lose less output as temperature rises.
