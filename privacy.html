Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information VivaVoltia ("we", "us"), available at vivavoltia.com, collects when you visit the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you.
  • Usage data: our hosting provider and analytics tools automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring page, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Solar calculator: the numbers you enter in our calculator are processed in your browser and are not sent to us or stored.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to remember your consent choices, measure how the site is used, and show advertising. When you first visit, a consent banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice at any time with the privacy settings button on the page.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects usage data on Google's behalf. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google AdSense and Advertising

We show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

If you do not consent to personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use cookies only for purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregated reporting.

Consent Management

We use Usercentrics, a consent management platform, to collect and store your cookie choices. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before non-essential cookies are set.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • To understand which content is useful to readers.
  • To show advertising that funds the site.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some sharing of data with advertising partners for personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under certain US state laws; you can opt out using the consent banner or the links above.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email contact@vivavoltia.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data Retention

We keep emails only as long as needed to handle your request. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings, after which it is deleted automatically.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Links

Our articles may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so please review their policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact
