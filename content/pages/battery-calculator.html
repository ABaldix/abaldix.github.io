Unit counts must cover both the energy (kWh) and the power (kW) you need. Costs are before any state or utility battery rebates, and assume at least 5 kWh, the smallest common home battery. Always have an installer do a proper load calculation before you buy.

How This Battery Calculator Works

Each appliance has a typical daily energy use (kWh per day), a typical running power (watts), and for motors like air conditioners and well pumps, a startup surge. We add up the daily energy for your selections, scale it to the number of hours you chose, and add about 10% for losses. Then we check two limits for each battery: whether enough units store the energy, and whether they can deliver the power your loads draw at the same time.
