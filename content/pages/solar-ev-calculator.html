How the Numbers Work

Yearly EV energy = miles × kWh per mile × 1.1 for charging losses. Extra solar (kW) = that energy ÷ (sun hours × 365 × 0.8). Gasoline cost = miles ÷ mpg × price per gallon. Grid charging cost = EV energy × your electricity price. Once paid off, solar-charged miles cost almost nothing.
