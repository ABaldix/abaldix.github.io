How This Calculator Works

First, it converts your monthly bill into yearly electricity use (bill ÷ price per kWh × 12). Then it sizes a system that produces that much energy, based on the peak sun hours where you live. Savings count the solar you use at home at full price, and the solar you export at the credit your utility pays. Cost is system size × installed price per watt, minus any rebates you enter. Savings grow each year with electricity prices, and shrink slightly as panels age.
