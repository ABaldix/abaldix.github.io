How Accurate Are These Calculators?

They are planning tools, not quotes. They use state averages for electricity prices (EIA, August 2026) and sunshine (NREL), typical system losses, and 2026 installed prices. Your roof direction, shade, utility rate plan, and installer pricing can move the results by 20% or more. Use them to understand the ballpark and to sanity-check quotes, then compare at least three written proposals using our quote checklist.

For the rules in your state, including net metering, tax credits, and payback examples, see Solar by State.
