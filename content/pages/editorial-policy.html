Editorial Policy

VivaVoltia exists to help US homeowners make better decisions about solar. These standards apply to every guide, calculator, and update we publish.

Independence

The site is funded by display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertisers do not choose, review, or pay for our content, and ads are clearly separated from articles. We do not sell, install, or finance solar systems, and we do not sell leads or share your details with installers.

Accuracy

  • Facts about laws, tax credits, net metering, and incentives are checked against official sources such as the IRS, state utility commissions, and state energy offices.
  • Figures are labeled with their source and date. Prices are given as ranges or averages, never as quotes.
  • When information is disputed or changing, we say so plainly rather than picking the most attractive number.
  • Product comparisons use manufacturer specifications and published warranty terms. We do not accept free products or payment from manufacturers.

Our full methodology is described in How We Research Our Solar Guides.

Updates and Corrections

We review guides when rules, programs, or prices change, and the "updated" date on each article reflects the last review. If we get something wrong, we fix it promptly. Significant corrections that change a conclusion are noted in the article. You can report an error through our contact page.

Use of AI Tools

We use AI tools to help with research, drafting, calculations, and graphics. Every article is checked by our editorial team against the cited sources before publication, and the numbers in our examples come from transparent models that anyone can reproduce with our calculators.

Not Professional Advice

Our content is general information, not financial, tax, legal, or engineering advice. For decisions that depend on your situation, consult a licensed professional and get written quotes from qualified installers. See our Terms & Disclaimer.
