Sun hours are rounded state averages from NREL data; your roof's direction, tilt, and shade change real output. For costs and payback, use our solar payback calculator.

The Formula

System size (kW) = yearly kWh to cover ÷ (peak sun hours × 365 × system efficiency). Number of panels = system size in watts ÷ panel wattage, rounded up. For example, 900 kWh a month in a 4.5-sun-hour state at 80% efficiency needs about 8.2 kW, or 20 panels of 420 W.
