Is solar worth it in Louisiana? For bill savings alone, often not. Louisiana power is cheap, and since 2020 most utilities pay new solar customers only about 3¢ for the power they send to the grid. New Orleans is the main exception. Elsewhere, solar makes the most sense when it is small, sized to your daytime use, or paired with a battery for hurricane-season backup.

How Louisiana Credits Solar

In 2019 the Louisiana Public Service Commission (LPSC) replaced retail net metering for new customers. Since 2020, solar you use at home still saves the full retail rate, but every kWh you export is credited at the utility's avoided cost. The LPSC publishes these rates yearly, and for 2026 they are about 2.8¢/kWh at Entergy Louisiana, Cleco, and SWEPCO. Customers who installed before the change are grandfathered under the old rules for a set period.

The City of New Orleans regulates Entergy New Orleans itself. It has kept more generous net metering for residential customers, which makes solar much more attractive inside the city. Confirm the current terms with Entergy New Orleans before you buy. Read how net metering works for the basics.

Louisiana Solar Incentives in 2026

  • Property tax exemption. State law exempts residential solar from property tax, so the added home value is not taxed.
  • No state tax credit. Louisiana's generous solar tax credit ended in 2015.
  • Local programs. New Orleans has run income-qualified solar and battery programs; check with the city and Entergy New Orleans.
  • Federal credit. The 30% residential credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025.

Who Should Go Solar in Louisiana

New Orleans homeowners are the strongest candidates. Elsewhere, solar fits people who want backup power after hurricanes, plan to stay long-term, or use a lot of power during the day. A battery lets you store midday solar instead of selling it for 3¢ and keeps essentials running during outages, though it adds $10,000 or more; see whether solar works in a power outage. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Louisiana still have net metering?

Not at full retail value for most new customers. Since 2020, Entergy Louisiana, Cleco, SWEPCO and co-ops credit exports at avoided cost, about 2.8¢/kWh in 2026. New Orleans, regulated by its City Council, has kept more generous terms.

Is solar worth it in New Orleans?

It is much better than in the rest of the state because Entergy New Orleans has offered more generous net metering. A typical system there pays back in about 13 years under those terms.

Are there solar incentives in Louisiana?

Solar is exempt from property tax. The state tax credit ended in 2015, and the federal credit ended for 2026 installs.

How long does solar take to pay off in Louisiana?

About 15–22 years outside New Orleans in 2026, depending on system size; about 13 years in New Orleans.
