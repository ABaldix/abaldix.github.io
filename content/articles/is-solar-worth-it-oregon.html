Is solar worth it in Oregon? For many homeowners, yes, especially outside the cloudy Willamette Valley. Oregon's electricity is cheaper than average, but its two largest utilities still offer full net metering, and Oregon is one of the few states that still pays cash rebates for solar and batteries. Those rebates do a lot of the work the federal credit used to do.

How Net Metering Works in Oregon

Portland General Electric and Pacific Power credit exported solar at the full retail rate. Credits roll forward month to month, and the account is trued up once a year; unused credits at the true-up do not carry into the next year, so size the system to your annual use. Idaho Power, which serves part of eastern Oregon, moved new customers to net billing at lower export rates. Read how net metering works for the basics.

Oregon Solar Rebates in 2026

  • Energy Trust of Oregon. PGE and Pacific Power customers can get a solar rebate, reported as a flat $2,500 per home for standard customers in 2026, plus battery rebates per kWh of storage. Income-qualified households can get larger "Solar Within Reach" incentives. You must use an Energy Trust trade ally installer.
  • Oregon Solar + Storage Rebate Program. Run by the Oregon Department of Energy, it has paid up to $5,000 for solar and $2,500 for storage. It reopened on June 15, 2026 with about $1.1 million in limited funding, and rebates must be reserved by an approved contractor before installation. Ask whether funds remain.
  • Property tax exemption. The added value of a residential solar system is exempt from property tax.
  • Local programs. Some municipal utilities, such as those in Eugene and Ashland, run their own incentives.

Who Should Go Solar in Oregon

Solar is a strong fit in sunny central and southern Oregon and for homeowners who can capture both rebates. In the Portland area, check tree shading carefully, because tall conifers can cut output sharply. Pairing solar with a battery is worth a look if you lose power in winter ice storms or summer wildfire shutoffs, especially with Energy Trust's battery rebate. See how much a solar battery costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Oregon have net metering?

Yes. PGE and Pacific Power credit exported solar at the full retail rate with an annual true-up. Idaho Power customers in Oregon are on net billing instead.

How much is the Energy Trust of Oregon solar rebate?

For standard PGE and Pacific Power customers in 2026, it has been reported as a flat $2,500 per home, with larger incentives for income-qualified households and separate battery rebates. Confirm current amounts with an Energy Trust trade ally.

Is the Oregon Solar + Storage Rebate still available?

It reopened on June 15, 2026 with limited funding. Rebates must be reserved by an approved contractor before installation, so ask installers whether money remains.

How long does solar take to pay off in Oregon?

About 9 to 12 years for a typical purchased system with rebates in 2026, and longer without them or on shaded Portland-area roofs.
